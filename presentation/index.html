Habit, Routine & Ritual

Habit

Automatic and unconscious, done without intention. Nothing breaks if it's skipped.

Routine

Deliberate but functional, built for efficiency, safety, or order. Skipping it breaks something practical.

Ritual

Consciously performed and symbolic, carrying emotional and social meaning. Skipping it just feels wrong.

Habit Routine Ritual
Attention Automatic, unconscious Deliberate but functional Conscious, intentional
Purpose None beyond itself Efficiency, safety, order Meaning, transition, identity
Emotion Neutral Low Central

Marie, 24
Professional from Seoul, working in Stockholm. Bachelor's in Economics.

"Moving to a new country to work and being away from my family is a big adventure for me, so during my pre-sleep routine I try to relax and wind down."

Background Story

Marie is from Seoul in South Korea and recently moved to Stockholm together with a roommate for her career. It is important to her to establish a routine to get used to her new environment and the new time zone as fast as possible. To stay in contact with her friends and family, she uses her phone a lot. Before she goes to sleep she usually calls her parents or answers text messages from her friends. She also likes to scroll through social media but wants to reduce her screen time, because she feels guilty about it, especially before going to sleep since it has an effect on her sleep. Social interaction is very important to her, that's why she likes to chat with her roommate every evening. To wind down before sleep she usually watches a movie or a TV show.

Problems
  • Using her phone too much and feeling guilty
Goals
  • Wanting to stay connected to home
  • Reducing her screen time
  • Relaxing and calming down during her sleep routine by consuming media
Behaviours
  • Consuming media before going to sleep
  • Calling friends and family, spending time with roommate
